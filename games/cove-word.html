🔤 Cove Word

One word a day — the same word for the whole Cove.

How to play

Guess the 5-letter word in 6 tries. Tap the letters, then Enter. Green means the right letter in the right spot, gold means the letter is in the word but in another spot, and gray means it is not in the word at all. Any five letters count as a guess — no dictionary checks, this is the friendly version.

A new word arrives at midnight. Win and your farm earns shells — the fewer guesses, the bigger the prize (100 / 75 / 50 / 35 / 25 / 15). When you finish, you will see how the rest of the Cove did today, and how your neighbors fared.
