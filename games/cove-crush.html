How to play

Tap a tile, then tap a tile next to it to swap them. If the swap makes a match of 3 or more of the same tile in a row, they pop, the tiles above fall down, and new tiles drop in — chains can keep matching all on their own!

Match 4 in a row and you get a striped tile — match it and it clears its whole row or column. Match 5 and you get a rainbow Cove Wave tile — swap it with any tile and it clears every tile of that type at once.

Reach the target score before you run out of moves to clear the level. If there are no moves left, tap Shuffle to mix the board up.
