How to play

Tap a tile, then tap a tile next to it to swap them. If the swap makes a match of 3 or more of the same tile in a row, they pop, the tiles above fall down, and new tiles drop in — chains can keep matching all on their own!

Match 4 in a row and you get a striped tile — match it and it clears its whole row or column. Make an L or T shape and you get a wrapped candy — match it and it blows up the 3×3 around it, then blows up again! Match 5 and you get a Sprinkle Bomb — swap it with any tile and it zaps every tile of that type at once.

Some levels ask you to clear the jelly instead of chasing points — match candies sitting on the blue jelly squares to melt it away before your moves run out. Win with moves to spare and Sprocket spends them on a Shell Crush bonus round of free blasts!

Reach the target before you run out of moves to clear the level. Follow the Boardwalk Path on the map to replay any level you have unlocked. If there are no moves left, tap Shuffle to mix the board up.
