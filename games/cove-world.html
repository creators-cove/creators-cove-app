Every building earns shells while you are away — but its basket fills up, so come back and collect! Tap Collect to empty the basket, or Upgrade to earn even faster.

How it works: crops grow in real time, even while the app is closed. Buildings fill their baskets while you are away (up to 8 hours). Your farm is saved on this phone, just like the game coins — if you switch phones or clear the browser, it starts over.
