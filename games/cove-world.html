🌱 Your Farm

Tap a plot to plant. Tap a growing crop to water it — watering cuts the wait in half! Harvest when it sparkles, or it will wither if you leave it too long.

👋 Visit a Neighbor

Every farm has its own code. Share yours so Cove friends can drop by, peek at your village, and lend a hand — a visit waters their crops and leaves them 10 shells. Your farm itself still lives on your phone; visiting just shares a picture of it.

📋 Orders Board

The girls post orders here. Harvest what they ask for and you earn the normal shells PLUS the bonus!

🏠 The Village

Every building earns shells while you are away — but its basket fills up, so come back and collect! Tap Collect to empty the basket, or Upgrade to earn even faster.

🎡 Grand Projects

Giant builds for the whole village to save toward — each one speeds up every building you own.

🖼️ The Cove Gallery

Art prints wash ashore in the Cove's surprises — tap one when it bobs up! Collect all 30 for your board. Finishing a set pays a shell bonus, and a complete board earns a full-size download of any one print you choose.

🛒 Cove Supply Store

Spend shells on farm supplies and forever-upgrades. Kim added this one herself — she runs a tight farm.

How it works: crops grow in real time, even while the app is closed. Buildings fill their baskets while you are away (up to 8 hours). Your farm is saved on this phone, just like the game coins — if you switch phones or clear the browser, it starts over. The Orders Board pays bonus shells for harvesting what the girls ask for, a daily hello bonus waits every morning, and if you spot a crab scuttling across the screen — tap him, he drops shells! Every so often the Cove sends a little surprise, too — a golden crab, a bottle with a note from one of the girls, even a washed-up treasure chest. Some surprises are art prints for your Cove Gallery board — collect all 30 and a full-size print of your choice is yours to download.
