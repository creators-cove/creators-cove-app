How to play

Tableau: build down in alternating colours — a red 7 goes on a black 8. Only a King can start an empty column. Tap a face-up card to pick it up (and every card on top of it), then tap where it goes.

Foundations: build up by suit, Ace first, up to King. Tap a card, then tap its foundation pile — or tap a foundation to send a card home.

Stock: tap the deck to draw one card. When the deck runs out, tap it again to turn the waste pile back over.
