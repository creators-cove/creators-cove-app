⭐ Cove Quest ⭐

Run the beach, cross the boardwalk, swim the lagoon and glide off the pier. Stomp crabs, dodge jellyfish, and bring the Golden Shells home!

Pick a level
Pick your girl

Hold JUMP in the air to glide · In the water, tap JUMP to paddle · Finish a level to unlock the next — 5 levels in all!

How to play: run with the arrow buttons, jump with the JUMP button. Stomp crabs flat for bonus shells — a pinch just knocks you back, but fall in a gap and you splash back to the last checkpoint, minus a heart. In the lagoon, tap JUMP to paddle and mind the jellyfish. Off the pier's edge, hold JUMP to glide on your umbrella. Reach the lighthouse — or the old Beacon — to finish the level!
