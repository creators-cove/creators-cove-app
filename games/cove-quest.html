⭐ The Golden Shells ⭐

Run the beach, cross the boardwalk, swim the lagoon and glide off the pier to the lighthouse. Stomp crabs, dodge jellyfish!

Hold JUMP in the air to glide · In the water, tap JUMP to paddle

How to play: run with the arrow buttons, jump with the JUMP button. Stomp crabs flat for bonus shells — but don't let them pinch you, and don't get stung in the lagoon. Fall in a gap and you splash back to the last checkpoint, minus a heart. Off the pier's edge, hold JUMP to glide on your umbrella. Reach the lighthouse to finish!
