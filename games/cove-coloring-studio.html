🖍️ Cove Coloring Studio

Real pages from our coloring book — tap a color, tap a shape, it fills!

How to play: pick a color from the palette, then tap any shape on the page to fill it. The white swatch is your eraser. Your coloring stays put when you hop between pages, so take your time. When a page is done, tap Save my art and post your masterpiece in the group! These pages come from Creator's Cove: The Girls' Beach Adventure — our coloring book (the whole book is in the Cove Downloads, if you want all 60 pages for printing).
