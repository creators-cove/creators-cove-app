How it works in the group

Rip packs just for fun — the coins are make-believe and there's nothing to buy. Every pack has 5 cards. Most pulls are Common, some are Rare, a few are Epic — and a lucky few are Gold, and Gold cards glow.

Elite Pack costs more coins, but it boosts your Epic and Gold odds — and it always has at least one Epic or better inside.

Your pulls are how you earn your way toward prizes. Pull a Gold card, tap WINNER CARD, take a screenshot, and post it in the Creator's Cove Facebook group — that's your entry. Winners are picked from the screenshots posted in the group.

Run out of coins? Your next 100 free coins arrive tomorrow — when you hit zero, a countdown timer appears showing exactly how long is left until midnight. Come back tomorrow, tap to claim your 100, and you're ripping again. Your coin balance and your Vault are saved on this device, so they're right where you left them when you come back — your session scores start fresh each visit.
